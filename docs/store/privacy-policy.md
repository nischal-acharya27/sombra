# SOMBRA — Privacy Policy

*Effective: [date of publication]*

SOMBRA ("the game") is a free game for Android, published by
[your name as it appears on Google Play] ("we").

## What the game itself collects

**Nothing.** The game has no accounts and no sign-in. It sends nothing to us.
Your progress (the furthest gate you reached, your level, your best ranks,
and which tutorial screens you have seen) is stored only on your device. If
your device's Android backup is turned on, that progress is included in your
own backup with Google, which we cannot access. Uninstalling the game
deletes it from the device.

## Advertising

The game shows ads through **Google AdMob**. There are two kinds:

- an occasional full-screen ad between gates;
- an optional ad you can choose to watch after falling, to continue where
  you fell.

To serve and measure these ads and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK
collects and processes:

- your device's **advertising ID** and other device identifiers;
- your **IP address**, which may be used to estimate your general location;
- **ad interactions**, such as ad views and taps;
- **diagnostic information** about the app's performance.

This data is encrypted in transit and is handled by Google under Google's
own privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>. How Google uses
data from apps that use its services:
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.

**Your choices.**

- In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game
  asks for your consent before personalised advertising. You can change
  your choice at any time with **PRIVACY OPTIONS** on the title screen.
- On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID in
  **Settings → Privacy → Ads** (the exact menu varies by device).

## Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at
children under 13. Ad requests are marked as not directed at children. We
do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
