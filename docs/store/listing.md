# Store listing — draft for review

Drafted 2026-09-29, for the account holder to edit and submit
(`docs/PLAY-STORE.md` step 7). Nothing here is submitted anywhere.

Four constraints shaped every line:

1. **Declared 13+** (`DECISIONS.md` § Audience). The copy sells a skill-based
   action game to teens and adults. It avoids words that read as made for
   young children ("cute", "kids", "easy", "cartoon"), because Play can
   reclassify an app whose listing plainly appeals to children, and this
   game's pastel look already invites a second look.
2. **The source traditions are living religions**
   (`docs/SPEC-CAMPAIGN.md` § "A note on the source traditions";
   `docs/research/villain-roster.md`'s respectful-treatment flags). The
   figures are not "demons to slay". Each gate tells a figure's own story
   from its own text. Kaikeyi's gate has no fight, and the copy must not
   imply one. Shurpanakha's mutilation is not a selling point, and nothing
   here mentions it.
3. **Eleven gates, and the finale is not built.** The copy says so, and
   promises nothing the build does not have.
4. **No "no ads" claim, and nothing misleading about the ads.** The free
   restart is always there; the second wind is the ad.

## Full description (4000 characters max)

> The machinery that judges the dead has stopped, and the souls it should
> have moved on are spilling into the world. You are a hunter, licensed to
> walk into those breaches — the gates — and set them right.
>
> **Every gate is a story.** Each one is built around a single figure from
> the Mahabharata, the Ramayana and the Krishna stories, told from their own
> text: Shakuni's loaded dice, Bakasura on the road to Ekachakra,
> Duryodhana at the lake on the eighteenth day, Ravana in his own throne
> room, and more. Some you fight. One you only witness. Each speaks for
> themselves before it ends.
>
> **Combat you can read.** Every attack announces itself before it lands.
> Nothing hurts you by touching you — only by committing to a strike you
> could have seen coming. When you fall, you will know exactly why.
>
> - Slash, launch, juggle, dash and cast. A small moveset with a lot of depth.
> - A style meter that rewards variety, and pays you back in mana.
> - Call a lingering soul with PUKAR and it fights beside you. One at a time,
>   and it can fall.
> - Wardens with their own tells, phases and last words.
> - Touch controls built for a phone held sideways.
>
> **Eleven gates are open now.** The twelfth, and the campaign's ending,
> is on its way as a free update.
>
> Free to play. SOMBRA shows an occasional ad between gates. When you fall,
> you can always rise again from the start of the gate for free, or watch
> an ad for a second wind where you fell — once per gate.
>
> No account, no sign-in, no purchases. Your progress is saved on your
> device.

(About 1,500 characters, leaving room to add.)

## Screenshots: what to capture

Capture these from the installed app on a phone, landscape. Every frame
should read as a fight or a place, not a menu:

1. Gate 1, a Warden fight mid-telegraph (the tell is the game's identity).
2. A PUKAR chaya fighting beside the hunter.
3. A Warden's intro window, their own words on screen.
4. Ravana's throne room, or gate 11's palace (the most striking realm).
5. The style meter at a high rank, mid-combo.

Avoid the death screen (it advertises the ad), avoid Kaikeyi's gate, and
avoid any frame whose point is a figure's humiliation.

## What not to write, and why

| Don't | Because |
|---|---|
| "Slay the demons of Hindu myth" | Flattens figures the tradition itself argues about, and several are venerated today (Ravana in parts of South India and Sri Lanka) |
| "The complete saga" / "the full Mahabharata" | Eleven gates, no finale yet |
| "For all ages" / "kids" / "cute" | The declared audience is 13+; this is the reclassification risk |
| "No ads" / "ad-free" | Untrue |
| Any gate's twist or ending | The Warden's last words are the reward |
